# Problem Statement

# Difficulty Finding Parking in Cities

## Overview

Finding parking in busy urban areas has become one of the biggest daily challenges for commuters. Due to the rapid increase in the number of vehicles and limited parking infrastructure, drivers often spend several minutes searching for an available parking space.

This problem not only wastes time but also contributes to traffic congestion, unnecessary fuel consumption, air pollution, and increased stress levels among commuters.

---

## Why is this a Problem?

Drivers usually have no visibility into available parking spaces before reaching their destination.

As a result, they often:

- Drive around multiple streets searching for parking.
- Reach meetings and workplaces late.
- Waste fuel unnecessarily.
- Experience stress and frustration.
- Increase overall traffic congestion.

---

## Target Audience

The primary users affected by this problem include:

- Daily office commuters
- College students
- City residents
- Visitors to commercial areas
- Delivery drivers
- Taxi and ride-sharing drivers

---

## Business Opportunity

A smart parking solution can improve urban mobility by providing real-time parking information, navigation, reservation, and digital payment facilities.

Such a solution benefits both users and parking providers by improving efficiency and maximizing parking utilization.
