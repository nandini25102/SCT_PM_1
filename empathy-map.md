# Empathy Map

Understanding what the user says, thinks, does, and feels helps identify pain points and opportunities for product improvement.

---

# 🗣️ Says

- "I'm getting late."
- "Parking shouldn't be this difficult."
- "There has to be an easier way."
- "Why can't I know parking availability before arriving?"

---

# 💭 Thinks

- I hope I find parking nearby.
- I don't want to waste more fuel.
- This is becoming frustrating.
- I might miss my meeting.

---

# 🚗 Does

- Opens Google Maps.
- Drives around nearby streets.
- Checks multiple parking lots.
- Asks security guards for parking availability.
- Parks farther away when no nearby parking is available.

---

# 😊 Feels

- Frustrated
- Stressed
- Anxious
- Impatient
- Tired

---

## Key Insight

The biggest challenge is the lack of real-time visibility into available parking spaces, forcing users to spend valuable time and fuel searching for parking.
