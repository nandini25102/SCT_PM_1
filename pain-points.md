# User Pain Points

## 1. Lack of Real-Time Parking Information

Drivers have no reliable way of knowing whether parking spaces are available before reaching their destination.

---

## 2. Time Wastage

Searching for parking often takes 10–20 minutes during peak hours.

---

## 3. Fuel Consumption

Driving around repeatedly increases fuel usage and travel costs.

---

## 4. Traffic Congestion

Vehicles searching for parking contribute to unnecessary traffic in busy areas.

---

## 5. Stress and Frustration

Late arrivals and uncertainty create anxiety, especially during office hours or important appointments.

---

## 6. Limited Digital Parking Facilities

Many parking lots still lack digital booking and payment options.

---

# Opportunities

These pain points indicate opportunities to build a smart parking platform that provides:

- Live parking availability
- Navigation assistance
- Parking reservations
- Cashless payments
- Smart notifications
